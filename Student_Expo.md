## DATA: ABSTRACT

Throughout data structures we  encountered multiple problems that provide
opportunities to try different  data structures. Multiple data structures
have multiple  uses. The data  structures that we  got to use  are singly
linked  lists, doubly  linked lists,  stacks, queues,  and binary  trees.
These were put to use in the collaborative creation of the infrastructure
for a game project.

## DATA: PERSONAL STATEMENT

Building  on  earlier  learning,  this class  introduced  important  Data
Structures  concepts and  allowed new  experience to  be gained  creating
larger and  more sophisticated programs.  With these new  experiences and
abilities,  we  had  the  opportunity  to pursue  an  activity  of  great
interest: the collaborative creation of  a game, utilizing our collective
knowledge,  refining our  ability,  and gaining  new  experience. It  was
impressive to be able to learn AND have fun doing so.

## DISCRETE: ABSTRACT

Discrete  Structures   introduced  us   to  increased   mathematical  and
algorithmic possibilities,  especially related  to logic,  which provided
useful insight  into tackling  more complicated problems.  In combination
with the  experiences from  Data Structures,  we were  able to  apply our
newfound  programming abilities  in  the creation  of  a game,  utilizing
both  the infrastructure  created  and our  new  abilities to  manipulate
information.

## DISCRETE: PERSONAL STATEMENT

Starting on our Computer Science journey, we began with learning to code.
Our programs were  simple, and small. Yet the detail  overwhelming. As we
continued to  grow and expand  our abilities,  our programs were  able to
grow  larger and  become more  sophisticated. Our  ability to  handle and
manage increasing  amount of  interconnected details  grew as  well. This
game  effort is  a  culmination  of our  evolution  and growth,  reaching
milestones by our own hand that were  once deemed too advanced and out of
reach.
